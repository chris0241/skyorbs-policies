Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Shadow Ltd ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use Sky Orbs ("the App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information relating to users of the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly require you to provide personal information such as your name, postal address, email address or telephone number in order to play the App, unless a particular feature of the App specifically requests this information.

However, the App uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, which may automatically collect or process certain information when you use the App.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • device type, model and operating system
  • device and advertising identifiers
  • approximate location derived from information such as your IP address
  • information about interactions with advertisements
  • diagnostic, performance and technical information
  • information relating to your use of the App

The precise information collected may depend on your device, operating system, location, privacy settings and consent choices.

2. Google AdMob and Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information from your device in order to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted and where you have provided any required consent, personalise advertising.

Google may use technologies such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses and other device identifiers when providing advertising services.

You can find further information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services in Google's privacy information:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove Ads", processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details: Google handles the transaction and may process information such as your Google account, purchase history and device information in accordance with its own privacy policy.

4. Personalised and Non-Personalised Advertising

Depending on your location and the choices available to you, advertisements displayed within the App may be personalised or non-personalised.

Personalised advertising may use information about your interests, activity and device to select advertisements that may be more relevant to you.

Non-personalised advertising is not selected on the basis of a profile of your interests, although information such as your approximate location, the content being viewed and certain device information may still be used for purposes including ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, security and fraud prevention.

Where required by applicable law, we will request your consent before using information for personalised advertising.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Users in certain regions, including the United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Switzerland, may be shown a consent or privacy message when using the App.

Where applicable, you can choose whether to consent to the processing of information for purposes such as personalised advertising.

Where required, the App provides a way for you to review or change your privacy choices.

You may also be able to control the use of your device's advertising identifier and advertising personalisation through the privacy settings provided by Android, iOS or your device manufacturer.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

These services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Games
  • Google Play Billing (for the optional "Remove Ads" purchase)

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party service providers. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for further information about how they collect and use information.

7. Why Information Is Processed

Information collected through the App or by third-party services may be processed for purposes including:

  • operating and maintaining the App
  • displaying advertisements
  • measuring advertising performance
  • providing personalised advertising where permitted
  • analysing App performance
  • diagnosing crashes and technical problems
  • maintaining security
  • preventing fraud and abuse
  • complying with legal obligations

8. Data Retention

We retain information that we collect directly only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by law.

Third-party service providers, including Google, determine their own retention periods for information that they process. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for further information.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers where necessary to operate, support or monetise the App. In particular, information may be processed by Google and its advertising technology partners in connection with advertisements displayed through Google AdMob.

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, legal process or a valid request from a public authority.

10. International Data Transfers

Third-party service providers may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, such transfers are subject to the safeguards required by relevant data protection laws. Further information about Google's international data processing arrangements can be found in Google's Privacy Policy.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child contrary to applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights relating to your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • request access to your personal information
  • request correction or deletion of personal information
  • object to or restrict certain processing
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • request data portability
  • complain to your relevant data protection authority

These rights are subject to applicable law and may not apply in every circumstance.

For information processed independently by Google or another third-party provider, you may need to exercise your rights directly with that provider.

13. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information under our control. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including where we change the features of the App, add or remove third-party services, or where legal or regulatory requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
